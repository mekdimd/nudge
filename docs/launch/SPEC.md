# Nudge launch film

Show how a goal replaces several precise clicks. The hook uses a website before the product reveal. Three tasks follow: download a bank statement, search for a tire-changing video, and draft an email with a separate Send approval.

## Creative direction

A continuous 42-second fixture story made the product bar dominate the film. The v2 cut uses a short task montage with recorded target pages. An architecture explainer would hide the actions, so it is outside this cut.

The user authorized iteration through delivery and requested about 30 seconds, faster motion, music and fewer labels.

## Picture and sound

1920 × 1080, 60 fps, 1,800 frames. Scene cuts fall at frames 202, 269, 673, 1,077 and 1,615. The canvas uses #eef2f5 and #222b36; Nudge retains its actual blue pointer. Outfit headlines are 74 px. The closing address is 64 px. Key copy holds after a 0.22 to 0.25 second entrance. Cursor flights take 0.24 to 0.34 seconds, with a 0.07 second press and 0.16 second rebound. Red rings mark failed clicks in the opening.

The Cloud Dancer excerpt is 30 seconds, edited with opening and closing fades and normalized to about -18 LUFS. No voiceover. Music rights and attribution are in ASSETS.md.

## Recorded behavior

The real Nudge task loop, Jev client and Gemini writer operate an isolated Chrome browser through a recording adapter. Bank and mail pages contain fictitious data. The bank downloads a real sample PDF. Mail stores a local sent-message result without contacting an email service. YouTube is a public search page.

Draft and confirmation panels come from the actual Qt application. The recording script approves sample prompts; the film shows those decisions with an ordinary black cursor. Blue cursor overlays reconstruct the chosen target positions from the action trace. They are editorial animation, not captured native cursor motion. Waiting between decisions is trimmed. The captures establish these browser-adapter results and do not establish native macOS or Windows support for the sample workflows.

Browser recordings have 25 fps sources. The edited clips and delivery file use constant 60 fps; cursor and camera animation render at 60 fps. Source frame repetition does not add detail to browser state changes.

## Banned defaults

No invented Nudge controls, live-performance claims, offline corner labels, ornamental status text, Live Caption demonstration, slow hook cursor, drifting headlines, gradient washes, glass cards or feature-card grid. Target pages must occupy most of each task shot. Keep product controls and proportions intact when cropping or enlarging them.
