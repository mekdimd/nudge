# Launch storyboard

The film follows one focal point at a time. Each demo begins with the task above a large Nudge window. Processing stays on Nudge. Browser actions get the entire stage, with a blue cursor and a slight camera move toward its target. Captions occupy the same position throughout the demos.

1. Three quick misses happen on the bank website. Red click marks show where the person missed. The website dims, then "Hard to click?" appears in the center. A blue cursor arrives, clicks Statements and carries into the reveal as the website fades away. Follow the cursor throughout this transition.
2. The blue pointer stops beside "Meet Nudge." The title settles, then holds. The pointer keeps the same position, scale and artwork across the cut.
3. "Say 'Hey Nudge' to start." Nudge's actual bar changes to listening, its orb responds and the request appears as a partial transcript. "Then say what you need" guides the viewer into the spoken bank request. Focus on the bar, with no browser competing for attention.
4. "Download a bank statement." The goal appears in the large Nudge bar. "Nudge finds the next control" accompanies its loading indicator. Focus moves to the bank website for Statements, View statement and Download PDF. Each cursor arrival has a visible click. The downloaded result holds, then Nudge's completed step feed closes the demo.
5. "Find an Excel tutorial on YouTube." Start with the request in Nudge, then show it processing. Focus moves to YouTube's search field while Nudge types the productive query. A blue trail follows the pointer into the suggestion click. Results appear before the completed Nudge feed.
6. "Enable Peek to see the controls." Introduce the full Nudge bar, then show the complete Peek button in close-up. The person clicks it and its enabled state appears. Focus moves to the VS Code issue tracker, where the actual control outlines draw across the page. "Peek outlines what Nudge can see" explains this view. Focus returns to the large Nudge feed and its loading indicator while "Jev is TypeSafe AI's action model" identifies the model. Its Labels decision appears in the feed. The browser returns with a spotlight on Labels. The outlines fade before the blue pointer clicks it, and the Labels page holds with its complete header.
7. "Write an email to your professor." Introduce the mail client, then show Compose opening. Focus moves to Nudge while it chooses the next steps and generates the draft. "Edit the draft before Nudge types it" appears above the complete review panel. A subject-field close-up shows the black cursor selecting "Absent" and replacing it with "Missing," with typing sounds. The complete edited draft returns before the person chooses Type it once. Focus moves to the mail client under "Nudge types it for you." Its recipient, subject and body fill in sequence, then the complete email holds. Focus returns to Nudge for "Approve sending the email." The person chooses Yes, send once. The browser returns under "Then Nudge clicks Send," the blue pointer clicks Send and the local result holds.
8. Nudge's pointer, wordmark and repository address close the film. The tagline has two lines: "Computer control for" and "limited dexterity."

The cursor flights remain quick. The longer cut gives the task, processing, approvals and results time to register. The film uses edited timing and reconstructed target movement. Bank and mail contain fictitious data, and no email is sent. The wake shot replays voice callbacks into the actual UI and loads the repository's Hey Nudge model. It does not test a microphone or speech recognition.
