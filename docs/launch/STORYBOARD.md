# Launch storyboard

The README also explains desktop app launching through a labelled Dock mockup and local vision through an orange OCR overlay. These still images sit in Photos and do not add shots to the film.

The film follows one focal point at a time. Bank, YouTube and email requests use blue chips with typed text to distinguish the person's goal from Nudge's explanations. Each demo begins with the task above a large Nudge window. Processing stays on Nudge. Browser actions get the entire stage, with a blue cursor and a slight camera move toward its target. Captions occupy the same position throughout the demos.

1. Three quick misses happen on the bank website. Red click marks show where the person missed. The website dims, then "Hard to click?" appears in the center. A blue cursor arrives, clicks Statements and carries into the reveal as the website fades away. Follow the cursor throughout this transition.
2. The blue pointer stops beside "Meet Nudge." The title settles, then holds as the two-line tagline appears. The pointer keeps the same position, scale and artwork across the cut.
3. "Say 'Hey Nudge' to start." The highlighted phrase appears word by word beside animated voice bars. Nudge's actual bar changes to listening, its orb responds and the request appears as a partial transcript. "Then say what you need" guides the viewer into the spoken bank request. Focus on the bar, with no browser competing for attention.
4. "Download a bank statement." The goal appears in the large Nudge bar. "Nudge chooses the next action" accompanies its loading indicator. Focus moves to a continuous bank recording for Statements, View statement and Download PDF. Each cursor arrival has a visible click. A green "Statement downloaded!" badge and the completed step feed close the demo.
5. "Play an Excel tutorial on YouTube." Start with the request in Nudge, then show Gemini drafting the query under "Gemini handles natural language." Focus moves to YouTube's search field under "Nudge enters your search query" while Nudge types. A blue trail follows the pointer into the suggestion and tutorial clicks. The selected tutorial plays under a green "Video playing!" badge.
6. "Curious how Nudge works?" Introduce the Nudge bar, zoom toward Peek and click it in the same window. The browser's debug boxes draw under "Nudge draws debug boxes around UI items." Guided close-ups show the controls. Return to Nudge's loading feed under "Jev is TypeSafe AI's action model," then its recorded decision under "Jev chooses the next action." Spotlight Labels before the cursor moves. A floating probability card appears over the browser under "Jev ranks options and selects the highest probability." Candidate bars grow, Labels wins in mint and the alternatives dim. A mint connector links Labels to its screen target. The completed result holds for half a second. The card exits, then the cursor resumes and clicks Labels. The scores are labelled "Example probabilities."
7. "Write an email to my professor." Introduce the mail client, then show Compose opening. Focus moves to Nudge while it chooses the next steps and generates the draft. "Nudge asks before typing" accompanies the spoken draft prompt. "Edit the draft before Nudge types it" follows above the review panel. A subject-field close-up shows the black cursor selecting "Absent" and replacing it with "Missing," with typing sounds. The complete edited draft returns before the person chooses Type it once. Focus moves to the mail client under "Nudge types it for you." Its recipient, subject and body fill in sequence, then the complete email holds. Focus returns to Nudge for "Approve before sensitive actions" and its spoken Send prompt. The person chooses Yes, send once. The browser returns under "Then Nudge clicks Send," the blue pointer clicks Send and the green sent confirmation holds for about a second.
8. Nudge's pointer, wordmark and repository address close the film. The tagline has two lines: "Computer control for" and "limited dexterity."

The cursor flights remain quick. The longer cut gives the task, processing, approvals and results time to register. The film uses edited timing and reconstructed target movement. Bank and mail contain fictitious data, and no email is sent. The wake shot replays voice callbacks into the actual UI and loads the repository's Hey Nudge model. It does not test a microphone or speech recognition.
