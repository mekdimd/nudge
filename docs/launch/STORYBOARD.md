# Storyboard

1. A cursor misses three controls on the bank website. Red rings and crosses mark the misses. "Hard to click?" fades in at the center after the clicks.
2. Nudge's blue pointer arrives beside "Meet Nudge." The reveal holds for about 1.8 seconds after the animation lands.
3. The bank stays on the left while Nudge runs on the right. The pointer selects Statements, the October document and Download PDF. The website reports the download.
4. Nudge types "Excel pivot tables for beginners" into YouTube's search field, chooses the matching suggestion and holds on the tutorial results. Keyboard sounds accompany the typing. The actual Nudge feed remains beside the browser.
5. "Meet Jev, Nudge's action model." A real VS Code issue list fills the browser. Actual Peek outlines reveal its controls, Jev chooses Labels, and the pointer opens the Labels page. Nudge's feed shows the decision.
6. Compose opens in the mail client. Focus slides to Nudge for almost four seconds of Jev choosing a step and the writer generating the draft. A black cursor selects "Absent" in its subject and replaces it with "Missing," one character at a time. The edited draft holds, then the person chooses Type it once.
7. Focus slides to the mail client. "Nudge types it for you" appears above the browser. The blue cursor moves through the recipient, subject and body as each field fills. The complete email holds for about two seconds before focus returns to Nudge.
8. Nudge's Send confirmation holds alone. The person chooses Yes, send once. Focus slides to the mail client, where the blue pointer clicks Send. The local result holds for more than two seconds.
9. The pointer logo, Nudge wordmark, "Assisted computer control" and GitHub address hold through the fade.

The 52.9-second film keeps the cursor flights quick and gives each demo more time. The browser camera zooms 10% toward cursor targets. Click pulses, typing sounds and Nudge tones identify visible actions. A faint blue trail follows Nudge's cursor flights and fades after arrival. The black cursor represents misses and sample approvals; the blue cursor reconstructs recorded targets. Waiting is trimmed and typing is retimed for readability. The email capture holds real model results briefly to keep processing indicators visible. Bank and mail are local samples, and no email is sent. The film remains understandable when muted.
